Your 5-step start

01 · Find itOpen Courses and choose Python Forge.
02 · Sign inSign up or log in with your invited email.
03 · MatchYour classroom assignment activates automatically.
04 · DashboardOpen My Classrooms or My Courses.
05 · BuildRead, code, run, save, and complete.

Where to find your learning experience

  1. Open your SkillSphere Dashboard.
  2. Look under My Classrooms or My Courses.
  3. Select Python Forge, then choose Start Learning or Continue Learning.
  4. Use the Course Content list to move lesson by lesson.

What you will do inside Python Forge

  • Follow clear explanations and worked examples.
  • Complete hands-on activities and knowledge checks.
  • Use the embedded Code Lab to write and run Python.
  • Save your work and return to the latest version anytime.

Recommended four-week study plan · 3–5 focused hours each week

Week 1 · FoundationsExpressions, variables, and reusable code.
Week 2 · Control + dataDecisions, loops, collections, and files.
Week 3 · CompositionFunctions, decorators, errors, and modules.
Week 4 · PortfolioApplied projects and your strongest work.

Can’t see your classroom?

  1. Confirm that you used the exact email your instructor invited.
  2. Sign out, sign back in, and refresh your Dashboard.
  3. Check both My Classrooms and My Courses.
  4. If access is still missing, ask your instructor or classroom manager to confirm the email spelling and assignment.
Start small. Keep experimenting.Every saved lab is evidence of what you can do. You can leave and return anytime.
