## Course design anchors

- **The opportunity and the seat:** Chapter 1 frames the CAIO opportunity as a time-sensitive leadership gap and emphasizes evidence over credentialing; Chapters 2–7 define capability boundaries, the CAIO seat, compensation context, skepticism, and safety/governance.
- **Proof over permission:** Chapter 15’s “Proof Ladder” proposes five practical evidence artifacts: a measured before-and-after automation, public-company teardown, one-page AI policy, written vendor evaluation, and a results memo. The source stresses dated evidence, honest baselines, and an operational result rather than credentials.
- **Deployment craft:** Chapter 19 presents an eight-step loop: assess, pick one, map it, build-or-buy, pilot, measure, hand off, govern. It stresses selecting one process at a time, choosing low blast-radius initial work, mapping real operations before tools, shadow-mode pilots, measurement, ownership, and governance.
- **Client discovery:** Chapter 27’s “Empty Seat Map” uses public signals—repetitive-role job ads, response-time or invoice-error review patterns, weak chat experiences, and owners handling frontline work—to identify operational pain. The source cautions that a defensible diagnosis requires convergence of multiple signals.
- **30-day execution:** Appendix K divides the first month into: Week 1 process discovery/baseline, Week 2 human-supervised build and shadow run, Week 3 real-work rollout, runbook, and after-measurement, and Week 4 a results memo, internal ask, and a 90-day plan. It requires dated artifacts, an employed-or-consulting track choice, one industry, one real target business/process, and a recurring weekly time commitment.

## Four-week BootCamp alignment

1. **Week 1 — The CAIO mandate:** opportunity, role clarity, capability limits, safety posture, career/path self-assessment.
2. **Week 2 — Evidence and opportunity diagnosis:** Proof Ladder, public/inside shadow audit, process baseline, value arithmetic, one-page AI policy.
3. **Week 3 — Deploy responsibly:** eight-step deployment loop, process map, tool criteria, pilot/shadow mode, measurement and governance.
4. **Week 4 — Lead, communicate, and scale:** results memo, leadership pitch, client/seat strategy, pricing/protection concepts, 90-day CAIO plan and capstone engagement brief.
